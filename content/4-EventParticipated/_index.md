This section presents the events I participated in during the **First Cloud Journey internship program**. These events provided opportunities to learn from experienced speakers, explore practical technology topics, connect with other participants, and improve both technical and professional skills.

During my internship, I participated in two **FCAJ Community Day** events. Each event focused on different topics related to artificial intelligence, cloud computing, software engineering, productivity, and career development.

Through these activities, I gained additional knowledge beyond the practical AWS labs and had the opportunity to learn from real-world experiences shared by technology professionals.

### [Event 1](4.1-Event1/)

&emsp;**Event Name:** FCAJ Community Day  

&emsp;**Date & Time:** 09:00, May 9, 2026  

&emsp;**Location:** 26th Floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, 02 Hai Trieu Street, Saigon Ward, Ho Chi Minh City  

&emsp;**Role:** Attendee  

&emsp;**Main Topics:** Learning psychology, dopamine management, Prompt Engineering, career mindset, and the BDM Method for software development  

&emsp;**Brief Description:** The event included four main sharing sessions about improving learning motivation, using AI tools effectively, developing a positive professional mindset, and organizing software projects through a structured working method.  

&emsp;**Value Gained:** I learned how to improve concentration using the Pomodoro Technique, create more effective AI prompts, maintain a growth mindset, and organize software development activities more systematically.

### [Event 2](4.2-Event2/)

&emsp;**Event Name:** FCAJ Community Day  

&emsp;**Date & Time:** May 23, 2026 — Full-day event  

&emsp;**Location:** Ho Chi Minh City  

&emsp;**Role:** Attendee  

&emsp;**Main Topics:** The future of software engineering in the AI era, Context Engineering, Amazon Q, Amazon CloudFront, product development during a 36-hour hackathon, and LLM consistency  

&emsp;**Brief Description:** The event connected current AI trends with practical software engineering and cloud computing topics. The speakers presented real-world experiences, technical demonstrations, and examples of how AI can support product development, enterprise operations, and cloud infrastructure.  

&emsp;**Value Gained:** I gained a better understanding of the continuing role of engineers in the AI era, the importance of business context when using AI, the use of Amazon Q in enterprise environments, CloudFront performance and security benefits, and the need for consistency and human review in production AI systems.

Participating in these events helped me expand my knowledge beyond the internship labs. I improved my understanding of AI-assisted development, cloud architecture, software project organization, and professional career development.

The events also gave me opportunities to listen to practical experiences, connect with other members of the technology community, and develop a more responsible approach to using AI and cloud services in real-world projects.
