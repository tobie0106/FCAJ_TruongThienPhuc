Event 1

Summary Report: “FCAJ Community Day - 09/05/2026”

Event Overview

FCAJ Community Day 2026 was a technology community event that brought together software developers, students, artificial intelligence learners, and people interested in developing their careers in the technology industry.

The event was organized to create an environment where participants could exchange practical knowledge, improve professional skills, and learn from the experiences of other community members.

With more than 200 participants from different companies and organizations, the event provided valuable opportunities for learning, networking, and discussing current topics related to software development, artificial intelligence, productivity, and career growth.

Event Objectives

  • Share practical knowledge about learning and working effectively
  • Explain how motivation and dopamine influence productivity
  • Introduce effective Prompt Engineering techniques
  • Provide guidance on developing a positive career mindset
  • Present the BDM Method for organizing software development activities
  • Create networking opportunities for technology learners and professionals

Main Sessions

The event consisted of four main sessions:

  • Hack Dopamine and Learning Psychology
  • Effective Prompt Engineering
  • Career Mindset
  • BDM Method for Software Development

Each session focused on a different aspect of personal development, technology, and professional working methods.

Key Highlights

Hack Dopamine and Learning Psychology

This session explained how dopamine affects motivation, concentration, and the feeling of satisfaction when completing a task.

The speaker discussed how modern sources of instant entertainment, such as social media, short videos, and mobile applications, can reduce the ability to focus on activities that require long-term effort.

Key topics included:

  • Understanding the relationship between dopamine and motivation
  • Dividing large goals into smaller and achievable tasks
  • Creating continuous motivation through short milestones
  • Limiting distractions from smartphones and social media
  • Developing positive and sustainable study habits
  • Rewarding progress after completing important tasks

A practical method introduced during the session was the Pomodoro Technique, which divides work into focused periods followed by short breaks.

Participants were also encouraged to organize their working environment and reduce unnecessary distractions to improve concentration.

Effective Prompt Engineering

The second session focused on how to communicate effectively with artificial intelligence tools such as ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini.

The quality of an AI-generated response depends greatly on the clarity and completeness of the user’s prompt. A well-structured prompt should clearly describe the context, objective, requirements, and expected output.

Important techniques included:

  • Providing sufficient background information
  • Defining a specific objective
  • Assigning a clear role to the AI
  • Describing the expected response format
  • Using examples through few-shot prompting
  • Dividing complex tasks into smaller prompts
  • Connecting multiple prompts through prompt chaining
  • Reviewing and improving prompts based on previous results

Participants practiced creating prompts for several common tasks, including:

  • Summarizing documents
  • Generating and explaining source code
  • Analyzing data
  • Writing technical content
  • Suggesting solutions for project problems

The session showed that AI tools can improve productivity, but users must still review and validate the generated results.

Career Mindset

The third session discussed the attitudes and ways of thinking required to develop a sustainable career in the technology industry.

Technical knowledge is important, but career development also depends on adaptability, continuous learning, communication skills, and the willingness to overcome challenges.

The main ideas included:

  • Developing a growth mindset
  • Believing that skills can improve through practice
  • Accepting mistakes as part of the learning process
  • Learning from unsuccessful results
  • Continuously updating technical knowledge
  • Receiving feedback with a positive attitude
  • Building professional relationships
  • Maintaining a balance between work, learning, and personal health

Participants completed reflection activities related to career challenges and discussed possible ways to respond to difficult situations.

The session helped me understand that failure should not be viewed only as a negative result. It can also provide useful experience for improving future decisions.

BDM Method for Software Development

The final session introduced the BDM Method, a structured approach that combines business requirements, development planning, project management, and process optimization.

The method emphasizes that software development should begin with an understanding of business needs rather than immediately focusing on technical implementation.

The main stages included:

  • Business Analysis: Identify business needs, user expectations, and project requirements
  • Development Planning: Define the scope, timeline, resources, and implementation plan
  • Management and Execution: Assign tasks, manage progress, and coordinate team members
  • Monitoring and Optimization: Track results, identify problems, and improve the working process

The BDM Method can provide several benefits:

  • Improve development productivity
  • Reduce unnecessary project costs
  • Control implementation time more effectively
  • Improve communication between team members
  • Increase product quality
  • Ensure that the final solution meets actual business requirements

Key Takeaways

Learning and Productivity

  • Motivation can be improved through proper task organization.
  • Large objectives should be divided into smaller milestones.
  • Reducing digital distractions can improve concentration.
  • Consistent habits are more reliable than temporary motivation.
  • The Pomodoro Technique can support better time management.

Artificial Intelligence

  • AI should be treated as a supporting tool rather than a complete replacement for human thinking.
  • Effective prompts need clear context, objectives, and output requirements.
  • Few-shot prompting can help AI understand the expected result.
  • Complex problems can be handled more effectively through prompt chaining.
  • AI-generated content should always be reviewed before being used.

Career Development

  • A growth mindset helps individuals respond positively to challenges.
  • Mistakes and failures can provide valuable learning opportunities.
  • Continuous learning is necessary because technology changes rapidly.
  • Communication and networking are important for professional growth.
  • Maintaining a healthy balance supports long-term career development.

Software Development Process

  • Software projects should begin with business and user requirements.
  • A clear development plan helps reduce confusion and delays.
  • Work should be monitored and adjusted throughout the project.
  • Structured processes can improve quality and team coordination.
  • Technical solutions should create practical value for users and businesses.

Applying to Work

After attending the event, I planned to apply the knowledge gained to my internship, study activities, and future projects.

Improving Personal Productivity

  • Use the Pomodoro Technique during study and development sessions
  • Divide large assignments into smaller tasks
  • Set clear milestones for each project stage
  • Reduce smartphone and social media usage while working
  • Review completed work at the end of each day

Using AI More Effectively

  • Provide clear context when writing prompts
  • Describe the expected output format
  • Include examples when the task is difficult to explain
  • Divide complex requirements into several smaller prompts
  • Review and verify AI-generated information
  • Use AI to support coding, documentation, and idea development

Developing a Professional Mindset

  • Remain patient when facing technical problems
  • View mistakes as opportunities for improvement
  • Ask mentors and team members for feedback
  • Continue learning new technologies and working methods
  • Participate in community events to expand professional relationships

Organizing Software Projects

  • Analyze project requirements before selecting technologies
  • Prepare a clear development plan
  • Assign responsibilities to each group member
  • Monitor progress regularly
  • Adjust the plan when problems occur
  • Review the final result based on user and business requirements

Event Experience

Attending FCAJ Community Day 2026 was a valuable experience that provided knowledge in several areas beyond technical development.

Practical and Accessible Content

The sessions used practical situations and familiar examples, making the topics easier to understand. The combination of explanations, discussions, and exercises helped participants apply the knowledge instead of only listening to theoretical presentations.

Learning About Productivity

The session about dopamine helped me better understand why motivation and concentration can change during the learning process.

I learned that organizing tasks, reducing distractions, and creating small milestones can help maintain productivity more effectively.

Improving AI Usage Skills

The Prompt Engineering session showed me that the effectiveness of AI tools depends greatly on how users describe their requests.

By providing clear instructions, context, and examples, users can receive more accurate and useful responses.

Career Orientation

The Career Mindset session encouraged me to develop a more positive attitude toward challenges.

It reminded me that professional development requires continuous learning, patience, feedback, and the ability to adapt to changes.

Understanding Structured Project Management

The BDM Method provided a practical framework for organizing software projects.

It helped me understand the relationship between business analysis, development planning, project execution, and process optimization.

Networking Opportunities

The event created opportunities to meet technology professionals, students, and members from different companies.

Through discussions and networking activities, I was able to hear different perspectives and learn from the experiences of other participants.

Lessons Learned

  • Personal motivation can be managed through suitable working habits.
  • Concentration improves when unnecessary distractions are reduced.
  • AI tools become more valuable when prompts are specific and structured.
  • AI-generated results must be reviewed and verified.
  • A positive mindset supports long-term professional development.
  • Software development should begin with an understanding of business needs.
  • Organized processes improve productivity and collaboration.
  • Technology communities provide valuable opportunities for learning and networking.
